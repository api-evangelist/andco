# Andco

> Andco is a Y Combinator (Spring 2026) startup building an AI-agent platform that automates the pre-litigation case workup process for personal injury law firms. Its agents retrieve and synthesize case documents across fax, phone, email, mail, web portal, and SMS channels — police and accident reports, insurance coverage verification, medical records, and medical bills — then draft demand letters and calculate damages. Andco covers auto accident, premises liability, medical malpractice, workplace injury, and mass tort practice areas.
